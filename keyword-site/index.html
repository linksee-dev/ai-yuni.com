Yuni Keyword Research

Yuni Keyword Research is a keyword research app that shows how many people search Google for a keyword every month. Give it a list of keywords and it returns the average monthly search volume, the volume for each of the last 12 months, the advertiser competition level and the top-of-page bid range for each keyword.

It is made by Yuni, an independent developer, to plan content and landing pages for Yuni's websites.

How it works

  1. Sign in with the Google account that owns a Google Ads account and allow access to Google Ads.
  2. Enter keywords, one per line, and choose a country (for example United States) or worldwide.
  3. The app calls the Google Ads API Keyword Planner and returns the search volume data.
  4. Results are saved as keyword notes in the research workspace, where they can be sorted and compared.

Google data the app uses

The app requests a single Google OAuth scope, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords, and uses it only to read keyword planning metrics from the Keyword Planner. It does not create, change or delete campaigns, ads, budgets or billing settings, and it does not access Gmail, Drive, Contacts or any other Google product. The app does not generate images or video. See the Privacy Policy for how data is stored and deleted.
