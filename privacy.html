Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Yuni ("we") collects, uses, stores, shares and deletes information on ai-yuni.com and in Yuni Keyword Research, an internal keyword research tool that uses the Google Ads API.

1. Information we collect

Website visitors. ai-yuni.com has no accounts or forms that collect personal data. Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may record standard request logs such as IP address, browser type, referring page and time of visit for security and basic traffic statistics.

Yuni Keyword Research. When the app is authorized with Google OAuth it receives:

  • An OAuth access token and refresh token for the Google account that authorized it.
  • The Google Ads customer ID of the account used for the request.
  • Keyword planning metrics returned by the Google Ads API: average monthly searches, monthly search volumes, competition level and top-of-page bid ranges for the keywords we submit.

The app requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords scope. It does not read campaign, ad, budget, billing, conversion or audience data, and it does not access any other Google service.

2. How we use the information

Google Ads API data is used only to look up keyword search volume for Yuni's own websites and to save those numbers in Yuni's private keyword research notes. It is not used for advertising, profiling, selling, or to develop, train or improve AI or machine learning models.

3. How we store and protect it

OAuth tokens and keyword results are stored only on the operator's own computer, in files readable only by that user account. They are not uploaded to any server or third-party service. Requests to Google are sent over HTTPS.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, transfer or disclose Google user data to anyone. The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Retention and deletion

Keyword metrics are kept in the research notes for as long as they are useful for the project and can be deleted at any time. OAuth tokens are kept until access is revoked. You can revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions; revoked tokens stop working immediately and are then deleted from the local configuration. You can also email us to request deletion of any data related to you.

6. Children

This site and the app are not directed to children under 13 and do not knowingly collect their information.

7. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.
