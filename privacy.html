Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy covers ai-yuni.com and the internal tools Yuni operates under this domain, including the Yuni keyword research tool that connects to the Google Ads API.

What we collect

The website does not require an account. Standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) may be kept by our hosting provider for security and analytics.

Google user data

The Yuni keyword research tool is used only by Yuni to look up keyword search volume for Yuni's own websites. When you authorize it with Google, it requests the Google Ads API scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords) and uses it only to call the Keyword Planner endpoints. It does not read, change or create campaigns, ads, budgets or billing data.

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the operator's own computer. Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access invalidates the stored token. You can also email us to ask for deletion.
